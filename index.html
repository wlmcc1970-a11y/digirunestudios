Straight Answers

Who is DigiRune Studios?

DigiRune Studios is a veteran-owned mobile app studio based in Beaufort, South Carolina. We develop premium apps for tabletop gaming and everyday use, and we partner with game publishers to create their officially licensed companion apps.

Are your companion apps official?

Yes. Every DigiRune Studios companion app is developed under an official license from the game's publisher, with rules and content reproduced exactly as published. Each purchase directly supports the game's creators.

Do your apps work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Our companion apps and flagship titles are available on the App Store & Google Play. Some new titles launch on Google Play first and arrive on iPhone later, and each app's listing shows where it is available today.

Do I need to create an account?

It depends on the app. Some work entirely without an account. Others use a secure sign-in with your existing Apple or Google account to store your data and keep it in sync across your devices. DigiRune Studios never receives your password.

Do your apps work without internet?

Yes. Our apps are built to keep working without a connection once installed. Features that rely on an online service, such as scanning new pages or syncing across devices, need a connection.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Use the Report a Problem form on this site, or email will@digirunestudios.com. Every message is read and answered personally.
