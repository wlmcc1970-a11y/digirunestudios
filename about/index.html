About DigiRune Studios

A veteran-owned mobile app studio based in Beaufort, South Carolina, developing premium apps for tabletop gaming, everyday use and the publishers behind the games.

Who We Are

DigiRune Studios develops and publishes mobile apps on the App Store & Google Play. The studio creates original titles for tabletop gaming and everyday use, and partners with game publishers to develop their officially licensed companion apps.

Every app is developed, published and supported in-house, from first build to long-term maintenance.

Our Founder

Will McCulloughFounder

Will McCullough founded DigiRune Studios in 2025 to bring a higher standard of quality to tabletop and everyday apps. He leads every project and approves every release.

Will is a U.S. Marine Corps veteran who served as a Combat Engineer, Senior Drill Instructor and Close Combat Instructor-Trainer, roles that demand precision and accountability.

Beyond the Marine Corps, Will has built a career in business and public service. He founded one of South Carolina's first mixed martial arts gyms and served as Governor-appointed Chairman of the South Carolina State Athletic Commission, where he brought the state its first UFC event. Will brings more than 25 years of business ownership to DigiRune Studios, including a franchise brokerage he built and led within one of the world's largest real estate brands, with more than 1,000 clients served.

His leadership roles include chapter President of BNI, Vice Chairman of a regional Parks and Recreation Board, three terms as an elected Director of his regional REALTORS association, and National Director for GAMMA USA, the U.S. branch of the global governing body for amateur mixed martial arts.

A lifelong tabletop gamer, Will brings that same discipline and experience to every app DigiRune Studios publishes.

How We Work

Every DigiRune Studios app is built under the Forge, our written quality standard for every release.

Source-exact content.

Licensed rules and text are reproduced exactly as the publisher wrote them.

Independent review.

Every build is reviewed independently before it is submitted to either store.

Full device testing.

Every app is tested on phones and tablets before release.

Long-term support.

Updates, customer support and store-review responses for the life of every app.

The founder makes every product decision and approves every release.
