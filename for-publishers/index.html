From Agreement to Launch

  1. Agreement. We discuss your game and sign a straightforward license agreement.

  2. Development. We design and build a fully interactive companion: searchable rules, rosters and builders, virtual card decks, generators and trackers, all drawn exactly from your published content. You review and approve before release.

  3. Launch and support. The app goes live on the App Store & Google Play under your game's name. We handle updates, customer support and quarterly statements.

Our process is built to take a companion app from signed agreement to both stores in about six weeks. Every game is different, and we keep publishers informed at every step.

Straight Answers

Is there any cost to the publisher?

No. DigiRune Studios covers the full cost of development, store publishing, hosting, maintenance and customer support. There are no upfront fees and no ongoing charges.

How are intellectual property and ownership handled?

The publisher retains full ownership of its game, artwork and trademarks. DigiRune Studios develops the app under an official license from the publisher and owns the app's software. Every partnership is set out in a written license agreement.

How is revenue shared and paid?

The publisher receives 30% of net revenue from app sales and in-app purchases, meaning what the App Store and Google Play pay after their fees, refunds and store-collected taxes. Payments are made quarterly, each with a detailed statement.

How is the app priced?

Companion apps are sold as a single one-time purchase, with no ads and no subscriptions. DigiRune Studios sets the price, and it never goes below $4.99 without the publisher's written approval.

What involvement is required from our team?

Very little. We ask for digital copies of your rulebooks and expansions, the artwork and logos you approve for use, and one contact person to review and approve content. Once the app is live, we recommend a launch announcement to your players followed by a quarterly mention, along with a link on your website. DigiRune Studios provides ready-made posts for each.

What support continues after launch, including updates and rule changes?

DigiRune Studios maintains every app for the life of the agreement, including updates for new phones and operating systems, customer support and responses to store reviews. When a publisher revises its rules, we work together to bring the app up to date, with every change reviewed and approved by the publisher.
